# ODLET — 60 s pitch video: voice-over script

Video: `marketing/video/out/pitch60.mp4` (music + SFX) and `pitch60-silent.mp4` (picture only, for a VO edit).
Render: `cd marketing/video && npm run render:pitch`. Composition: `src/pitch/Pitch60.tsx`.

About 125 words at a calm 2.2 words per second. Record in one take, then lay it over `pitch60.mp4` with the music ducked about 10 dB,
or over `pitch60-silent.mp4` with your own bed. Keep the numbers exactly as written; they match the deck.
